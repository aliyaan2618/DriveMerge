DriveMerge

All your cloud accounts,
one storage space.

DriveMerge merges your Google Drive, OneDrive, Dropbox, Mega and 60+ other cloud accounts into a single space with their combined free storage. Free and open source.

Download for Windows Source on GitHub

Windows 10 and 11. macOS and Linux are coming. Windows may show a SmartScreen warning the first time: click "More info", then "Run anyway".

Pool your space

Add up free and paid accounts. Choose where new files go: most free space, fill one first, or spread evenly.

One file browser

Browse, upload, download, search and find duplicates across every account at once.

Sync and schedule

Mirror, update or two-way sync between your PC and the pool, on demand or on a schedule.

Command line

Run saved syncs or any rclone command on your pool from scripts.

Good to know

  • Pooling adds up space; it doesn't give any single account more room.
  • Your files stay in your own accounts. DriveMerge has no servers and never sees your passwords.
  • Removing an account from the pool takes its files out of the pool view. Nothing is deleted.
